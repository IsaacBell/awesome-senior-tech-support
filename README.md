## Remote Tech Support Services

- [Candoo](https://www.candootech.com/consumer-services) - Remote onboarding, quick tech support, learning materials, and scam-avoidance help; memberships are often bought by adult children for a parent. About $240/year for an individual, $360/year for a couple.
- [Senior Tech Navigators](https://seniortechnav.com/vip-tech-concierge) - Household-wide concierge support with unlimited emergency mini-sessions, quarterly in-home visits, and scam alerts. $119.99/month.
- [TechEase](https://techeasehelp.com/membership) - Recurring tech help and security monitoring for seniors, with monthly virtual support hours and in-home options. $79–$149/month.
- [Mittler Senior Tech](https://mittlerseniortech.com/concierge/) - Low-cost unlimited email-based support covering device setup, password help, and scam checks. $20/month.
- [Senior Concierge Services](https://scs.manofweb.com/) - Device, Wi-Fi, password-organization, and customer-service assistance for seniors and veterans. $199/year per household.

## Scam Protection & Digital Safety

- [Security for Seniors](https://securityforseniors.com) - Digital safety platform with free training, a security dashboard, breach checkups, data-broker removal, and family oversight tiers.
- [Sahai](https://sahai.care) - AI agents that monitor a senior's email, finances, and calendar by phone, detecting scams before harm is done.
- [MemoryLane](https://memorylane.co) - AI digital caregiver providing on-screen tech support, scam protection, and companionship, with free and paid tiers.

## Bill & Account Management

- [Golden](https://www.joingolden.com) - Financial account organization and alerts for older adults, with bill-pay available under a power of attorney. $20/month.
- [Kinage](https://kinage.co) - Bill, fraud-detection, and coordination support that keeps older adults in control with audit trails.
- [Steady Household](https://steadyhousehold.com) - Household management for aging adults: vendor scheduling, bill sorting, and family dashboards.
- [TendTo](https://tendto.ai) - Shared workspace for caring for an aging parent: bills, medications, appointments, and documents, with an emergency packet that syncs automatically.
- [Eazewell](https://eazewell.com) - Digital-inheritance and account management that identifies a person's full digital footprint and helps families close or transfer accounts.
- [Help Unlimited](https://www.dailymoneymanager.com/daily-money-management-services/bill-paying-management/) - Bill-paying assistance that works with the client's power of attorney, CPA, financial advisor, or attorney.
- [Baker DMM](https://www.bakerdmm.com/dmm-services/power-of-attorney/) - Durable power-of-attorney support for daily money management, explaining what banks require before working on a senior's behalf.
- [The L Crew](https://thelcrew.com/senior-concierge) - Senior financial concierge and daily money management serving South Florida in English, Spanish, Russian, and Ukrainian.
- [Senior Billing Services](https://www.seniorbillingservices.com/) - Bill-payment and caregiver-compliance services that monitor for unusual activity and prevent financial elder exploitation.

## Senior Living & Managed IT

- [TechForSeniorLiving](https://techforseniorliving.com/) - HIPAA-compliant managed IT for senior-living communities, starting around $1,870/month per community.
- [SeniorTechs](https://seniortechs.com/) - Premium managed IT for senior living and active-adult communities, with 24/7 live support and ransomware resilience.
- [Sentech Living Solutions](https://sentechlivingsolutions.com/) - Smart-home safety technology with professional installation and proactive monitoring for aging in place.

## Learning Resources & Programs

- [Senior Planet from AARP](https://seniorplanet.org/classes) - Free technology training classes for older adults, covering smartphones, online safety, and everyday digital skills.
- [Tech Savvy Seniors (NSW Government)](https://www.nsw.gov.au/community-services/seniors-card/tech-savvy-seniors) - Free, structured technology training program for seniors, from the New South Wales government.
- [Cyber-Seniors](https://cyberseniors.org) - Nonprofit connecting volunteer tech mentors with older adults learning digital skills.
- [LeadingAge — Technical Support Programs](https://leadingage.org/resident-client-technology-guide/) - Guidance for agencies that serve older adults on building technical support programs.
- [Senior Source — Technology Education](https://theseniorsource.org/senior-technology-education-bridging-generation-gap/) - Plain-language explainers on technology education for older learners.
- [Senior Tech Connect](https://srtechconnect.com/) - Organization-facing programs for teaching older adults practical tech skills and building confidence after class ends.

## Voice Dictation & Accessibility

- [Apple Voice Control (iPhone/iPad)](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111778) - Hands-free dictation and device control for seniors who find typing difficult.
- [Siri](https://www.apple.com/siri/) - Voice assistant for calls, messages, and app control without needing to read a screen.
- [Google Voice Access (Android)](https://support.google.com/accessibility/android/answer/6151848) - Spoken commands to navigate an Android phone.
- [Apple Accessibility](https://www.apple.com/accessibility/) - Display adjustments, larger text, and "reduce motion" settings that make phones easier for older eyes.
